Delete your One Step Closer account

One Step Closer · Last updated: 29 October 2026

You can ask us to delete your One Step Closer account and all online data linked to it at any time.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open One Step Closer and sign in.
  2. Tap the ⚙️ settings button, then your account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then tap again to confirm. Your account and all online data are deleted right away.

Or request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to igorisandrijanovas@gmail.com with the subject Delete my One Step Closer account.
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to the game, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We confirm by email and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email address and password).
  • All your characters and their online game saves.
  • Your leaderboard and challenge entries, and your numbered event finds.

What is kept

Nothing is kept on our servers after deletion. The game save on your own phone is not touched; you can remove it by resetting progress in the game's Options or uninstalling the app.

See also our privacy policy.
