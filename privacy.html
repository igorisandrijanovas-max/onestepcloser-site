One Step Closer: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

One Step Closer is a walking game: your real steps power your character's progress. This policy explains what the app reads, what it stores, and your choices.

Step data

  • Health Connect (read only). With your permission, the app reads your step count from Health Connect. It does not read any other health data and never writes to Health Connect.
  • Step sensor. With the Physical activity permission, the app counts steps from your phone's step sensor while the game runs, including in a background service shown by a notification.
  • Steps are used only to run the game: experience, item finds, adventures, goals and streaks.

What is stored

  • On your phone: your game save (character, items, progress, settings and daily step totals).
  • Online, only if you create an account: your email address and password login (handled by our hosting provider, Supabase), your character's game save, and for leaderboards and challenges your character name, level, total steps, number of collected items and active days, and which numbered event items your character found.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos (an optional profile photo is kept only inside your game save, and in your online save if you have an account), or advertising identifiers.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not use your health data for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers.
  • The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics.

Who processes data

Online saves and leaderboards are stored with Supabase (database and login service) on our behalf. Other players can see only what leaderboards and challenges show: your character name and game statistics.

Your choices

  • You can play fully offline without an account.
  • You can revoke step access at any time in Health Connect or Android settings.
  • You can reset all progress in the game's Settings.
  • To delete your account and online data, use Delete account in the game's account screen, or follow the steps on our account deletion page or email igorisandrijanovas@gmail.com. We delete it within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
